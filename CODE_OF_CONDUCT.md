# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone participating in this project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and artifacts, not people.
- Give actionable feedback with context.
- Respect privacy, licensing, and attribution.
- Welcome contributors with different experience levels and perspectives.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or bad-faith participation.
- Submitting content or assets that contributors are not authorized to distribute.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and participation that violate these standards. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's preferred GitHub contact channel rather than publishing sensitive details in a public issue.
